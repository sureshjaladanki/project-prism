# Vision

Give anyone in India a clear, non-partisan picture of the country from official government statistics — and nothing else.

The numbers already exist. They are split across Union ministries, states and Union Territories, PDFs, dashboards, and delayed releases. This platform gathers that official record into one portrait: what the state collects, spends, and delivers, and how people live. High or low, good or bad — the reader decides.

## Purpose

A shared, checkable picture of India that does not belong to a party, a ministry, or a news cycle.

## What this is

A civic data platform. It uses only public official statistics — surveys, censuses, administrative series, and budget figures produced by government statistical systems — and presents them so a citizen can see the country without a decoder ring.

It is inspired by the *role* of [USAFacts](https://usafacts.org/): make government data usable, do not tell people what to think. It is not a copy of that site, its topics, or its American frame.

## What this is not

- A newsroom, think tank, or advocacy shop
- A place for forecasts, private polls, or partisan scorecards
- A substitute for the agencies that collect the data
- A ranking of states or a report card on any government

## How we work

- **Official sources only.** Union and state statistical systems, the Census, the Reserve Bank of India, and other mandated public series. Cite the producer, the series, and the date.
- **Show, don’t spin.** Context and definitions are in; verdicts are out.
- **India as it is governed.** The Union, states, Union Territories, and districts — not a single national average pretending to be the whole country.
- **Trust the method.** When series break, lag, or disagree, say so. Do not paper over holes in the record.

## Who it is for

Citizens first. Then journalists, students, researchers, and anyone in public life who needs the same numbers, from the same sources, without a partisan filter.
