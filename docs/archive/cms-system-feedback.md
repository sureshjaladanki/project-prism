## Charter rulings (this batch)

0. **Reader first — relate and resonate (top priority).** We build for the reader in [vision.md](../vision.md): a clear, non-partisan picture of India from official government statistics ([vision.md](../vision.md) opening), for **citizens first**, then journalists, students, researchers, and public life ([vision.md](../vision.md) “Who it is for”). It is **paramount** that a citizen without the codebook can **relate to and resonate with** the answer page — a spoken thesis, evidence that moves (prior period, what changed, what the number *is*), not a half-decoded codebook of series disclaimers. Shape alone (question → fact-lede → evidence → method) is not enough if the voice still teaches the decoder ring above the fold. **Outranks** every other open F-row in this file (including `F-unit-system` / money scale) for Content Editor and Charter sequencing until C1–C3 pass a citizen read on resonance. If that requires changing **scope**, [vision.md](../vision.md), [design-philosophy.md](../design-philosophy.md), [editorial-guidelines.md](../editorial-guidelines.md), or [design-system.md](../design-system.md) — **do it**: Charter Editor leads; Content Editor drafts editorial/voice; UI/UX signs philosophy and look. **USAFacts adapt (no prior costume/shape-only veto).** Without restriction under this ruling, the crew may **choose, learn, and adapt** from [usafacts.org](https://usafacts.org/) and the live profile [usafacts-profile.md](../usafacts-profile.md) whatever serves that vision interest — answer voice, thesis-first lede, fast facts, ranks, evidence payoff, scan path, product moves, even look/chrome patterns — into **our** house for India. Prior “role only / not the kit” and “page that would pass for USAFacts with the wordmark swapped has failed” lines in standing contracts are **reopenable** here; they do not block adoption. Still required by vision: official Indian government statistics as the record; agency cite stays; no invented figures; high or low, the reader decides. Spin and a partisan report card are not resonance. Trust still owns record pass/block.

1. **One Indian display string, no leftover producer unit.** Compact K / L / Cr (ruling 6) is the *whole* citizen unit. Fail `"35.27 L crore"` and `"14.23 L Thousand"`: the scale token must not sit next to the vintage `unit` word. Population counts on that page use **Cr**, same concept, same scale. Grouping is Indian (12,34,56,789 or compact Cr/L/K). Vintage `unit` stays as printed.

2. **Cite line opens a panel; the card stays on the page.** First-screen cite and every chart `.source-line` may look like the compact source-line. The control opens the existing in-text cite `popover` (P3). `.source-stack` still holds the full cards. Do not add `/cite`. Do not leave the view. Do not drop a producer that is not already named next to a figure.

3. **Source-stack is the complete list.** “Sources on this page” may list **every** cite the answer-page uses, including those already named in the fact-lede or immediately after a chart. Completeness at the bottom is in. Do not treat an earlier in-text or chart source-line as a reason to omit the card from the stack. Those lines are the same panel control.

4. **Method is quiet by type, not by a second column.** Fine-print (smaller than answer body). Same `--desk` left/right edge as other sections. `--measure` on `.how-measured` yields. Quietness is size and placement after the record, not a narrower well.

5. **C2 fact-lede for a 2026 reader.** The first number answers “how many people live in India” with the official **projection for the current year, named as a projection**. Census 2011 stays on the same first screen as the last enumerated Total (stat row or the next sentence). Do not treat the projection as a census count. Do not treat 2011 as today. Methodologist signs the wording.

6. **Axis ticks are citizen period labels.** Census years are years (1901…2011), not producer footnote marks (`$`, `@`, `#`, `+`). Budget charts do not print machine prefixes (`end-`, `actual-`). Footnotes and BE/RE/Actuals live in method or the cite, not in the tick.

7. **One house unit system; drop `DisplayScale`.** Ingest, vintage, and render must share one **standard Indian numbering** contract (ones / thousand / lakh / crore as magnitudes of the *same* base). Observation `value` + denomination are the record; render **formats** for display (Indian digit grouping, compact K / L / Cr labels) and must not re-interpret magnitude by guessing a second scale from page peaks. **`DisplayScale` / `scale_for_concept` / `apply_scale` that divide already-crore (or already-thousand) values as if they were ones are out.** Fail `/money/union` showing Budget BE revenue as `0.35 Cr` when the vintage holds `~3.5e6` ₹ crore. Vintage `unit` stays as the producer printed it; citizen chrome never reprints leftover “crore of rupees” beside a scale token (ruling 1 still). Platform Architect owns the contract; Pipeline and Ingest keep denomination honest; Methodologist signs denomination notes; Trust fails wrong order-of-magnitude on ship. Yields to ruling 0 on *copy voice*; does not yield on wrong magnitude.

8. **One citizen gap phrase: “not published”.** Running copy, chart titles/subtitles, method, and house pages (`/how-this-works`) do not say hole, missing (as desk shorthand), or “do not fill.” Schema status stays `unknown` / `withheld` / … Citizen chrome uses **not published**. Published zero and a gap must stay verbally and visually distinct (`F-igst-gap-contrast`). Same as [citizen-page-feedback.md](citizen-page-feedback.md) ruling 5; this batch enforces it on house pages and leftover answer-page strings (`F-gap-phrase`).

9. **Home and sleeve teasers match the answer page.** `/` fast-facts and `/{sleeve}` one-liners use the same citizen lead and the same `display_string` as the slice hero — not a stale 2011 census teaser when the page leads with a 2026 projection, and not `L crore of rupees` when the page uses one compact unit (`F-home-teaser`).
