# C1 method notes — citizen vs desk

Persona: Methodologist. Desk fields unchanged: [c1-method-notes.md](../archive/c1-method-notes.md).

**Rule (once):** a citizen caveat says what the number is, never what a ministry should do. `citizen_note` is citizen. `do_not` is desk.

| Card | Desk (`do_not`) | Citizen (`citizen_note`) |
|------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| CPI General | Do not merge Rural/Urban; do not stitch 2012=100 | Headline retail price index, Base 2024=100; Combined as published; Provisional vs Final |
| CFPI | Do not chart Division 01 as food | Food index = Group 01.1, not Division 01 |
| Division/Group | Do not label Division 01 as food | COICOP 2018 divisions and groups; food-only is 01.1 |
| Linked back series | Do not present as independently compiled 2024-basket | Linked General on Base 2024=100, All India only; blanks are not zero |
