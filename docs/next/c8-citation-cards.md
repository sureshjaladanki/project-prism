# C8 citation cards

Slice: **C8**. Citizen question: What does the official record say about foodgrain, and what does the public food system deliver?

Producers on these cards: Directorate of Economics & Statistics / Economics, Statistics and Evaluation Division, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (DA&FW), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, for foodgrain **production** estimates; Department of Food & Public Distribution (DFPD), Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, for the monthly **Foodgrains Bulletin** (procurement, allocation, offtake, NFSA coverage tables as printed); Food Corporation of India (FCI), Coordination Division, for **central-pool stock** statements hosted on DFPD; National Food Security Portal (`nfsa.gov.in`) for the **ration-card administrative dashboard**.

**Keep Family A (farm production / procurement) and Family B (PDS / NFSA delivery) on separate charts.** Do not merge them into one “food security” series. Bulletin page 8 reprints MoA production with an explicit DES source line — that reprint is **not** the production source of record (Cards A1–A3 are).

Latest production on these cards: **Third Advance Estimates 2025-26** (as on **27 May 2026**); sealed Final Estimates **2024-25** (as on **20 November 2025**); state/UT Area–Production–Yield workbook aligned to the 3rd AE vintage. Latest DFPD Foodgrains Bulletin: **August 2026** (upload **16 September 2026**). Latest central-pool stock PDF on DFPD: **as on 01.09.2026** (prepared **09.09.2026**). Verified **22 September 2026**.

Districts are parked (charter-out).
