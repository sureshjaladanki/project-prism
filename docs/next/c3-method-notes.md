# C3 method notes — citizen vs desk

Persona: Methodologist. Desk fields unchanged: [c3-method-notes.md](../archive/c3-method-notes.md).

**Rule (once):** a citizen caveat says what the number is, never what a ministry should do. `citizen_note` is citizen. `do_not` is desk.

| Card | Desk (`do_not`) | Citizen (`citizen_note`) |
|------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| Tax revenue | Do not chart blank cess as 0; do not collapse BE/RE/Actuals | Union tax heads as printed; blanks not zero |
| Non-tax | Do not treat UT receipts as C5 | Union non-tax as printed |
| Capital receipts | Do not treat net debt as gross or as the stock | Capital receipts flow; debt stock is another card |
| Annex-1 trends | Do not hide PDF reconstruction | Multi-year receipts as printed |
| Expenditure | Do not issue waste/welfare verdicts | Central expenditure and transfers as printed |
| Deficit statistics | Do not grade FRBM from this table | Year-status deficit lines as printed |
| Liabilities | Do not present as general government including states | Central outstanding liabilities as printed |
| FRBM statements | Do not print a fake FRBM table | Statutory prose; not a reconstructable table |
| AFS | Do not treat UT-without-legislature lines as C5 | Laid Annual Financial Statement as printed |
| CGA monthly glance | Do not annualise April–July | Intra-year unaudited (@) figures to July 2026 |
| Finance Accounts | Do not silent-substitute for Budget BE | Statement No. 1 Actuals in crores as printed |
